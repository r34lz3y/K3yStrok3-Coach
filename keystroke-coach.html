How to play

Rogue programs ride light cycles toward your firewall. Each one carries a word. Type the word to lock on and fire a disc that derezzes it.

Clean streaks build your combo. Every 10 in a row raises your score multiplier, and every 20 earns an EMP that clears the grid. Every fifth wave brings a rootkit with a longer phrase. Beat it to restore a shield.

Your game keystrokes feed the same weak-key stats as the speed test, so playing still sharpens your drills.
